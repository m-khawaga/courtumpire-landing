Singles rules

  1. Play best of three sets (first to two sets). A regular set is first to 6 games with a two-game lead, unless the configured set tiebreak is reached.
  2. At 6–6 games, play a set tiebreak to at least 7 points, win by two. This tiebreak completes the set as 7–6.
  3. At one set each, replace the entire deciding set with a match tiebreak to at least 10 points, win by two. Record it as [points–points], not set games.
  4. Set structure follows the cited ITF tie-break-set / short-set method. The social best-of-one choice and event arrangements are agreed by the organizer.
  5. Use advantage games: at deuce, win two consecutive points to win the game.
  6. Warm-up: 5 minutes. Scheduled breaks: 2 minutes between sets. These organizer arrangements may differ from competition rest limits.
  7. For standard tiebreak end changes, change physical ends after each six points. Serving half and physical court end are different.

Reporting results

Report the final result to the organizer before leaving the court.

Reference: ITF Rules of Tennis 2026, Rules 5–7, 10, 29 and Appendix VI. Timed/custom arrangements and additional notes are organizer-defined; this handout is not an official approval or certification.

Preset rules and organizer choices

ITF presets use advantage or no-ad games and tie-break sets. A set tiebreak completes that set; a deciding match tiebreak replaces the entire deciding set. Timed rules and edited/custom arrangements are labelled organizer-defined. These document settings do not add scoring modes to the singles scoreboard.

Example

Choose both disciplines with shared best-of-three rules and a 10-point match tiebreak at one set each. Or give doubles a separate 20-minute timed format with an explicit time-expiry and tied-result rule.
