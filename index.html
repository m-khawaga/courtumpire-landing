A few useful answers

Before you head to court.

How can I keep tennis score with CourtUmpire?

Set up your match rules and award points on your Android phone. CourtUmpire tracks points, games, sets, server order and side changes, with voice score calls and a live match log.

Can I score a tennis match from a Wear OS watch?

Yes. A connected Wear OS watch lets you award points and follow the server, games, sets and match information for the active match on your Android phone.

How do I correct a scoring mistake?

Use undo to correct an accidental point. Fast-tap protection also helps prevent repeated taps from adding points by mistake.

Can I export my tennis match results as a PDF?

Yes. CourtUmpire generates detailed PDF reports on your device. You choose whether to save, export or share a report using your selected app or service.

Do I need an account to use CourtUmpire?

No account is required in the app. Match records stay locally on your device, and the app has no ads. Optional website analytics is controlled separately through Analytics settings.
