CourtUmpire Weekly

Privacy & participation

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Reading an edition needs no account, analytics consent or engagement service.

Request deletion of your CourtUmpire data

To request deletion of server-held Weekly participation data, email info@courtumpire.com. Include the article URL or title, the nickname you posted under, and enough detail to identify your comment, such as its approximate date and a short extract. Nicknames are unverified, so we may ask for proportionate additional information to locate the record and check the request. Please do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

When we permanently delete an identified Weekly comment, we remove its text, nickname, optional commenter email and submission record, together with reports about that comment, reactions to that comment and their retry records. Hiding a comment is different: it removes public visibility but retains the record privately.

A moderation audit entry can remain after comment deletion. It records the action, comment identifier, time and a keyed identifier for the administrator, not the deleted comment text or email. Audit entries are removed at the next successful daily cleanup after 90 days, normally within 91 days. Unrelated short-lived abuse and retry records expire separately and are removed by successful hourly cleanup, normally within 24 hours. Failures or provider outages can delay physical cleanup.

Deleting one comment does not automatically delete other comments or separate article-level reactions. Tell us which additional records your request concerns. When reactions are enabled, you can change or remove an article reaction in the same browser; its local article token can remain for up to 180 days unless you clear this site’s data. Clearing the token does not itself erase a server-held reaction. A nickname or email alone does not identify every browser reaction. The email you send us to request deletion is correspondence, handled under the main policy’s email retention rules, rather than being erased automatically with a comment.

Private backup exports are subject to a 30-day manual deletion policy. Provider-managed recovery backups are not immediately purged when a live record is deleted and follow the applicable plan’s recovery window; this notice does not promise an independently verified exact backup-expiry date. Restored data must be purged before engagement reopens. Copies already downloaded, quoted or captured by other people may remain outside our control.

Native match history is different from server-held Weekly data. We cannot remotely erase the match history stored by the phone app or its companion devices through a Weekly deletion request. Delete saved matches in the app or clear its data using Android’s app settings, and manage system backups, device-migration copies and exported PDFs/PNGs separately. Sending us a deletion request does not clear those copies on your device.

Comments and moderation

If engagement is enabled, Cloudflare Workers and D1 process comments under an unverified nickname, together with submission time and optional email. Comments first enter a private human moderation queue. Only approved names, comment text and dates appear publicly. Email is private and optional, used only for a possible personal response. Providing it does not create an email subscription; we do not send email newsletters or automated comment emails. Android’s optional “New Weekly articles” alerts are separate: the app checks the public RSS feed and generates notifications locally. You can also add the public RSS feed to a reader of your choice. Neither Android alerts nor RSS reading requires a commenter email.

Cloudflare Turnstile helps prevent abuse when you choose to submit. It receives connection and device data separately from optional Google Analytics. Our API uses short-lived keyed network rate-limit identifiers; it does not retain raw IP addresses in the application database or fingerprint readers.

Reactions

A random article-scoped browser token remembers your current reaction to the article and each approved comment for up to 180 days. You can change or remove each reaction independently. Deleting a comment also deletes its reactions. This is a best-effort browser limit, not verification of a unique person. It is independent of advertising and analytics. Blocking storage may make reactions unavailable.

Retention and your choices

Daily cleanup deletes pending, rejected and spam comments, and optional commenter email, at the next run after 30 days (normally within 31 days). Audit metadata is deleted at the next daily run after 90 days (normally within 91 days). Approved and hidden comments and reactions stay until explicitly deleted. Published articles and private drafts/revisions stay until the owner explicitly deletes them. Separate hourly cleanup removes expired abuse and retry records; their application lifetime is limited to 23 hours so a successful hourly run removes them within 24 hours, independently of daily cleanup. Cleanup failures or overdue runs require recovery and are shown in private administration. There is no proactive email alert. A provider outage can delay physical deletion, so this is not an unconditional storage-expiry guarantee. Unpublishing or moving an edition to private Trash removes public reading and hides its engagement but does not erase moderation records. Restore leaves the edition unpublished until the owner explicitly republishes it. Permanent article deletion removes its stored article content and published revisions; comments and reactions require a separate explicit deletion in moderation. Independently saved drafts and exports are separate copies. Provider-managed backups are not immediately erased by application deletion. Private backup exports are subject to a 30-day manual deletion policy, separate from application cleanup; there is no automatic export or export-deletion service. Provider-managed backups follow the applicable plan recovery window and cannot be individually purged by this site; restored databases must be purged before reopening engagement.

Reports flag a comment for human review and do not automatically hide it. To request access, correction or deletion, contact Mahmoud Khawaga, identifying the edition and comment. Do not put private information in public comments.

External video

Videos are ordinary links unless embedding rights have been checked. An optional Load video button connects to YouTube only after your choice; no video thumbnails load first. Privacy-enhanced embedding still transfers data. Analytics consent remains a separate choice.
