RETENTION AND FAILURE LIMITATION REQUIRING EXPLICIT ACCEPTANCE AT ACTIVATION
Articles and private drafts/revisions remain until explicit deletion. Approved/
hidden comments and reactions remain until explicit deletion. Pending/rejected/
spam comments and all optional commenter emails expire at next daily cleanup after
30 days, normally within 31 days; audit metadata after 90, normally within 91 days.
Rate counters expire logically in 10 minutes; retry records in 23 hours. Independent
hourly cleanup removes expired records and anonymizes report network hashes after
23 hours, normally within 24 hours, without waiting for daily cleanup.
D1 does not provide automatic physical row TTL: provider outages/quota exhaustion
can delay erasure. An unconditional 24-hour physical deletion guarantee is NOT
implemented. Do not enable collection until the owner explicitly accepts this
operational limitation; otherwise a stronger expiry design remains a blocker.
Failed/overdue/missing cleanup is visible in private moderation; failures throw a
generic invocation error without PII. There is no proactive email alert. Owner must
check health daily and close submissions while recovery is pending. Restore then
purge before reopening. No promise of compliance is made by these controls alone.
Encrypted private exports expire after 30 days, managed manually by the owner.
Provider-managed D1 Free Time Travel is separately limited to 7 days; it cannot
be purged per-record. No automated encrypted export destination/job is activated.
