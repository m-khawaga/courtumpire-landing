4 players · Beginner · 14 minutes

Pressure points with a team budget

Choose when to use a limited bonus point and discuss a simple plan with a partner.

Match format · Doubles teamwork · Serve and return · Minimum 6 minutes

Equipment
rackets, balls
Space
One tennis court with clear run-off space
Suggested block
14 minutes; allow at least 6
Open practice planner →

Drill instructions

Purpose & setup

Choose when to use a limited bonus point and discuss a simple plan with a partner.

Start A+B against C+D using normal doubles boundaries. Each pair has one optional bonus call per eight-point round. Agree the intended serve/return plan aloud before the point.

How to play

  1. Play exactly eight points, serving two points each in order A → C → B → D. Alternate deuce/ad within each service turn and allow two serve attempts.
  2. Before any point either pair may say “bonus”, spending its one bonus call for this round. A point is worth two only if that calling pair wins it; otherwise the opponents receive the usual one.
  3. Play out each point with volleys or one bounce; a double fault, miss, out ball or second bounce loses it. Both pairs may call their own bonus on the same point.
  4. After eight points change partners AB/CD → AC/BD → AD/BC, reset bonus calls and restart the listed two-point serving order with the new partners placed on opposite ends. Finish the current point at time.

Scoring or success

Count one for each ordinary winning point and two for a calling pair winning its bonus point. Drawn rounds stand. This is a custom decision game, not official tiebreak scoring.

Rotation & roles

All four serve twice per round. Change partnerships after eight points; each pair chooses fixed deuce/ad receiving sides for that round.

Make it easier

Keep the eight-point rotation but remove the bonus; discuss one safe placement goal before each service turn.

Add a challenge

Before spending the bonus, the pair must state a concrete broad target and coverage plan; the bonus still depends only on winning the point.

Mixed levels

Use comfortable serving pace or an agreed underarm serve; keep the same bonus allowance and equal two-point service turns.

Safety & spacing

Discuss the plan only with a dead ball, keep partner swing areas clear and prioritise controlled placement over body shots.
