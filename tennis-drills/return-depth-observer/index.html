3 players · Beginner · 12 minutes

Return depth observation round

Separate return depth from point-winning with a dedicated observer and equal role turns.

Cooperative drill · Serve and return · Consistency · Minimum 6 minutes

Equipment
rackets, balls
Space
One tennis court with clear run-off space
Suggested block
12 minutes; allow at least 6
Open practice planner →

Drill instructions

Purpose & setup

Separate return depth from point-winning with a dedicated observer and equal role turns.

A serves behind the near baseline, B receives behind the far diagonal baseline and C observes safely outside the near sideline near the service line. T is the near backcourt behind that service line.

How to play

  1. A serves with two attempts. B returns a legal serve after one bounce, aiming beyond the near service line.
  2. End on the return’s first bounce. C calls only “deep”, “short” or “out”, using the visible lines, then reports B’s running total.
  3. Alternate deuce/ad sides for six attempts. A double fault counts as an attempt with no return score.
  4. Rotate A → B, B → C, C → A. Complete the dead-ball change before feeding and finish the current attempt at time.

Scoring or success

B earns one for an in-court return and another if it lands in T. C reports the two components separately out of six; the serve is not independently verified.

Rotation & roles

Six attempts per role. The observer rests from hitting and never retrieves until the ball is dead.

Make it easier

Use a gentle practice serve from closer to the service line and accept the whole near singles court.

Add a challenge

Call a broad left or right backcourt half before each serve.

Mixed levels

Adjust serving pace to the receiver; the observer gives factual depth feedback rather than coaching claims.

Safety & spacing

C stands beyond the doubles sideline and outside the return path, with room to retreat. Stop if the observer’s position obstructs play.

Follow the illustrated instructions. No video has been published for this activity.
