On court with 2

Tennis drills for 2 players
Make every minute count.

Make the most of a hitting partner with shared rally targets, straight and diagonal patterns, and short serve-and-return games.

Before your first ball.

Agree on pace and target size before you feed. Start closer together for consistency, then move back. Alternate who feeds, and make shared rally goals achievable for the less experienced player.
