3 players · Beginner · 10 minutes

Call the direction

Read a simple external cue and choose the correct broad groundstroke target.

Cooperative drill · Down the line · Crosscourt · Minimum 5 minutes

Equipment
rackets, balls
Space
One tennis court with clear run-off space
Suggested block
10 minutes; allow at least 5
Open practice planner →

Drill instructions

Purpose & setup

Read a simple external cue and choose the correct broad groundstroke target.

A starts behind the near-right baseline and B behind the far baseline with one ball. C stands safely outside the near sideline, observing and calling either “line” or “cross”.

How to play

  1. B gently feeds to A’s near-right groundstroke area. As the ball crosses the net, C calls “line” or “cross”.
  2. A plays after one bounce towards the called far backcourt half: right for line, left for cross.
  3. End on A’s shot’s first bounce. C confirms the chosen direction; nobody runs after a live ball.
  4. After six feeds rotate A → B, B → C, C → A. Mirror A’s corner after a full role cycle; finish the current attempt at time.

Scoring or success

One point for choosing the called half and one for landing inside that half. Count out of twelve, separating decision from accuracy.

Rotation & roles

Six feeds per role. The caller rests physically and supplies the decision cue; everyone takes that role.

Make it easier

C calls before B feeds so A has more preparation time.

Add a challenge

C calls just after the feed bounces, provided A still has comfortable reaction time.

Mixed levels

Give the less experienced hitter an earlier cue and larger target, retaining the same feed count.

Safety & spacing

C stays beyond the sideline with enough swing clearance. B keeps feeds comfortable and nobody deliberately wrong-foots a player beyond safe reach.
