2 players · Beginner · 12 minutes

Crosscourt rally challenge

Work together to keep a diagonal rally going.

Cooperative drill · Consistency · Crosscourt · Minimum 6 minutes

Equipment
rackets, balls
Space
One tennis court with clear run-off space
Suggested block
12 minutes; allow at least 6
Open practice planner →

Drill instructions

Purpose & setup

Work together to keep a diagonal rally going.

A stands near the near deuce corner and B near the opposite diagonal baseline corner. Use the diagonal halves of the singles court; begin nearer the service lines if needed.

How to play

  1. A drop-feeds gently crosscourt to B. Play after one bounce.
  2. Both send every ball back through the same diagonal, inside the singles sidelines.
  3. A net ball, out ball or second bounce ends the rally. Retrieve safely and reset.
  4. Alternate the feeder after each rally. Change to the other diagonal after five rallies. Finish the rally at the block end.

Scoring or success

Count consecutive legal rally shots together, excluding the feed. Try for five, then ten.

Rotation & roles

A and B swap feeding after each rally and mirror starting positions after five rallies.

Make it easier

Move closer and use a slower, comfortable ball speed.

Add a challenge

Use a smaller target while keeping the same rotation and scoring.

Mixed levels

Use a larger target for the less experienced player and a smaller one for the stronger player.

Safety & spacing

Keep spare balls and equipment outside playing routes. Call shared balls, leave room for swings and retrieve only after the ball is dead. Use a comfortable pace and stop rather than chasing into another player or court.
