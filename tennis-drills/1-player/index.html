On court with 1

Tennis drills for 1 player
Make every minute count.

A spare court and a little time are enough for a useful solo session. Alternate movement rehearsal and measured serve attempts; a practice wall is optional.

Before your first ball.

Start gently with shadow movements. Use the court lines as targets, keep loose balls clear and include retrieval in your practice time. Only choose the wall activity if a permitted wall and clear practice area are available.
