On court with 3

Tennis drills for 3 players
Make every minute count.

Three players can have a purposeful session on one court. Choose an all-active game or a fixed rotation so everyone knows when their next turn begins.

A turn for everyone.

Use the exact rotation in each activity. The challenger scorer rests for four points; the feeder observes for three rallies. Waiting players stay safely clear, and everyone changes position only while the ball is dead.
