App privacy at a glance

Native scorekeeping requires no CourtUmpire account and has no ads or analytics SDK. Player names, match settings, scores and history are stored by the app. A connected Wear OS companion exchanges match information with the phone. System backup, device migration and your own exports can create copies elsewhere. Explore opens online website content and checks the public Weekly feed; optional website analytics and Weekly participation are also available there. These web features do not automatically receive your native match history.

Match information

The phone stores player names, match settings, scores, match logs and saved history in app storage. The connected Wear OS companion displays match information, including player names and scores, and sends scoring, undo and status commands to the phone through Google Play services’ Wearable Data Layer. The phone controls the active match. This is companion-device communication, not a CourtUmpire cloud match account.

Android system backup and restore are enabled for the app. Its backup rules do not exclude match records or preferences. Depending on your Android version, device, backup provider and settings, eligible app data can be copied into system backups or transferred to another device and restored later. CourtUmpire does not provide its own match-backup server or give the developer access to your system backup. See Android’s backup information and your device’s backup controls.

Deleting a saved match, clearing app data or uninstalling removes the relevant local app copy. It does not necessarily erase system backups, copies on another device, or files you saved or shared. Manage backup and migration copies through the relevant device or backup service, and remove exported copies separately.

PDF reports and PNG result images

Reports and result images are generated on your device. Exporting a PDF can save a file in Downloads or the app’s document storage. Sharing a PNG result card opens Android’s sharing chooser and grants the app you choose access to that image. Explore’s supported Save PDF and Save PNG actions use the system document picker; printing uses Android’s print service.

A report or image may contain player names, scores, dates, match details or information you entered into a website tool. A destination you choose, such as a messaging app, cloud drive, email app or print service, may receive the file and associated delivery information and may store or forward it under its own rules. CourtUmpire does not automatically publish these files. Saved files and recipients’ copies can remain after you delete a match or uninstall the app.

Explore, Weekly reading and notifications

Explore displays CourtUmpire’s public website in an embedded Android WebView. Opening pages or refreshing online content makes HTTPS requests to our website and its delivery services. The app also requests the public Weekly RSS feed to obtain article titles, descriptions, links and publication dates. It keeps public article metadata in memory and can use a local feed cache where the server permits caching. Article read/dismiss state and notification preferences are stored locally. Website cookies and local storage inside Explore are separate from your ordinary browser and from native match history.

The feed can be checked when you enter or refresh Explore. If you enable “New Weekly articles” and Android permits its notification channel, Android WorkManager can also check in the background approximately every three hours when a network is available and the battery is not low. Android may delay checks or alerts. These alerts are generated locally after feed polling; they are not an email subscription or a remote push-notification service. Adding the public RSS feed to a separate reader is another choice, governed by that reader’s settings.

“Live match score” notifications are a separate, optional local display of the current native match, not Weekly alerts. Notifications can reveal player names, scores, or article titles and excerpts on the notification shade or lock screen, depending on Android’s settings. Use the app’s separate “Live match score” and “New Weekly articles” controls, Android notification/channel settings, and lock-screen privacy controls to choose what is shown. Turning Weekly alerts off stops scheduled article checks; it does not stop a feed request when you deliberately open or refresh Explore.

Optional Google Analytics and Weekly participation can be available on website pages viewed inside Explore, just as on the website. Before acceptance, optional analytics stays off. In Explore’s compact footer, open “Privacy & legal” and then “Analytics settings” to review or withdraw that WebView’s analytics choice; in an ordinary browser, use “Analytics settings” in the footer. See the analytics section below. Native notification permission is a separate choice and does not consent to analytics or comments.

Weekly reading needs no participation. If you choose to comment under an unverified nickname, react or report a comment, the separate Weekly privacy and participation notice explains the information involved, moderation, optional private email, anti-abuse services, retention and deletion. Providing a commenter email does not subscribe you to email updates or Android article alerts.

Local website tools and club logos

Practice plans, scoring histories, result-card drafts, rotation settings, separate session/organizer checklists and event details are processed in your browser. Where available, browser local storage remembers them until you remove them or clear this site’s data. They are not uploaded to CourtUmpire or Google by these tools. Sharing a downloaded or printed document is your own action; its contents may include the names and event details you entered.

An optional PNG, JPEG or WebP club logo is decoded, resized and re-encoded locally. Only the processed image and its dimensions are saved locally; the original filename and embedded metadata are not retained by this logo feature. The selected logo is reused in local document previews and prints. Remove club logo clears that saved image. If storage is unavailable or full, the current page remains usable but changes may not persist. Clearing analytics consent does not erase your checklists, event details or logo.

Website access data

The website is published through GitHub Pages. Cloudflare provides DNS and proxies delivery and security for courtumpire.com; Weekly pages, its public feed and participation services also use Cloudflare Workers, with Weekly content and participation data stored in Cloudflare D1. Website requests, including those from Explore, expose technical connection information such as IP address, browser/device information, requested address and access time to the services handling the request. GitHub can process origin requests, and Cloudflare processes requests delivered through its network. DNS resolution, content delivery and security are separate from optional Google Analytics and can occur before any analytics choice.

See GitHub’s privacy statement and Cloudflare’s privacy policy. These services may process data internationally. This notice does not promise that all delivery data stays in the EU or specify an unverified provider log-retention period.

Your saved tools and local quiz

Practice plans, match configuration and point/undo history, event settings, optional processed club logos and both checklist templates can be saved in this browser where storage is available. Checklists include text, completion state and temporarily removed reminders. Permanently deleting a reminder removes it from that saved checklist; restoring defaults can bring back predefined reminders, but not deleted custom text. Reset score clears match scores and undo history while retaining configured names and format. These local records are not automatically sent to us.

Use the planner’s Clear saved session, checklist remove/delete actions, score reset and logo remove controls as appropriate. You can remove all stored website data through your browser’s site-data settings; this also clears other locally saved CourtUmpire preferences and tool data. The quiz randomizes and scores in memory without visitor identifiers, accounts or persistent quiz history; refresh starts a new run. Optional analytics receives only approved categories and bounded question/score totals after consent, never your submitted answer text or saved tool contents.

Email enquiries

The Contact page opens your own email app. Selecting a topic prepares an email subject; the website does not send a message, upload an attachment or submit a contact form. The copy button copies only our public contact address. We do not store draft enquiries in this website’s browser storage.

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, message and any attachments, together with ordinary email delivery metadata. We use it to handle your enquiry and respond. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

For general enquiries, the intended legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in responding, subject to necessity and balancing. Article 6(1)(b) applies only where the enquiry is necessary for a contract or steps you request before a contract; applicable statutory rights requests may require processing under Article 6(1)(c). Optional website analytics consent is not required to email us.

Email to our contact address is routed through Mailgun mail servers and the configured receiving email services. Mail delivery or forwarding passes the message and attachments to the receiving mailbox; it is distinct from that mailbox’s storage and deletion. See Mailgun’s privacy information. We retain correspondence while needed to resolve the enquiry and necessary follow-up, and longer only where justified by applicable legal duties or a dispute. Clearing website or app data does not delete mailbox messages, copies held by your email service, or backups. Email handling may involve processing outside the EEA; no EU-only processing or fixed provider-retention guarantee is made here.

Depending on the applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact info@courtumpire.com to exercise your rights. You may complain to a competent data-protection supervisory authority, including where you live or work or where an alleged infringement occurred.

Optional website analytics

This production website loads optional Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-H387MYJ9KJ) only after you select “Accept analytics”. Google Analytics is a service from Google; for users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland, Google identifies Google Ireland Limited as the provider of its services. After your consent, we use analytics to understand page visits, scrolling, outbound-link and file-link clicks, supported embedded-video engagement, form interactions, tool starts, successful plan generation, match completion, PNG generation, print-dialog requests, checklist action categories, validation/failure categories, selected navigation destinations, local quiz starts/completion totals, confirmed score resets, drill-use categories, contact-intent categories/actions and clicks on links to Google Play, so we can improve the website. We do not count every scored point. A print-dialog request is not evidence of printing, and a generated export is not evidence that you saved or opened it. A Google Play click does not tell us that you installed the app.

After you accept, Google receives event information, a browser identifier stored in first-party analytics cookies, and technical information such as browser/device characteristics and connection information. Google states that Analytics uses IP addresses for coarse location and discards them before logging for users in the EU, Switzerland and UK; see Google’s regional data and privacy information. This does not mean that analytics is anonymous. Our custom event wrapper accepts predefined categorical values and bounded numeric counts only. It uses a known public page URL without query strings or fragments, a fixed generic analytics page title, an empty referrer and fixed placement/tool/format/action identifiers. It does not send player or team names, organizer names or email addresses, event names, reminder text, logos, logo filenames, document contents or raw error messages. Contact measurement records predefined topic categories and email/copy intent, including copy success or failure. It contains no name, email address, complete mailto URL or message content, and never records a successful message submission. Google may also process automatic session and engagement information and common technical fields. Automatic event detection is configured separately from our custom event wrapper. Depending on the enabled stream settings and supported content, Google can record scroll thresholds, outbound link destinations, file-link metadata, video metadata and form metadata such as form ID, destination and submit-button text. These automatic events are separate from our custom event allowlist; the enabled measurements have been checked to exclude entered text and personal information. A tool form interaction is not a contact enquiry or a confirmed lead; a file-link click is not proof that a file was saved. Contact remains email-based, with no Contact-form submission backend. Weekly comments are a separate optional feature described below.

Free-text searches and the words entered into tennis tools are outside our analytics scope. Automatic Site search is disabled, and verification confirmed that search terms are excluded from the measured events. Local tool forms use public destinations without visitor query strings in their form metadata.

Analytics cookies created by this integration use names beginning with cu_ga and help distinguish visits and sessions. Their configured lifetime is at most the remaining analytics consent period, without extending it on each visit. Browser settings may shorten this period. Advertising consent stays denied; Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled in the website configuration.

Before you accept, and after you reject, this integration loads no Google Analytics or Tag Manager script and sends no analytics requests, including cookieless consent pings. The website works without acceptance. Google may process information outside your country; see Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from sites using its services and Google’s explanation of Analytics cookies.

Remembering and changing your website choice

We remember explicit acceptance or rejection for 180 days using the local storage key courtumpire.analytics.v1 (record version 3, scope website-engagement-v3) and a first-party preference cookie named cu_consent. These contain your choice and its timing, not match data. Both must confirm current-scope acceptance before analytics starts on a later visit. Older rejections remain valid. Earlier acceptance covering page visits or custom tool usage does not cover the expanded automatic engagement measurement: you are asked to choose again. We do not queue actions before consent and send them later. Missing, invalid or expired choices do not authorize analytics. If your browser cannot save the choice, analytics stays off and we may ask again.

Use “Analytics settings” in any page’s footer to reopen the controls. Select “Reject analytics” to withdraw consent: tracking stops, accessible analytics cookies from this integration are removed, rejection is saved, and the page reloads if the Google script was started. If preferences cannot be saved, an analytics=off URL flag carries rejection through the reload and internal links to prevent stale acceptance from restarting tracking. You can also clear this site’s storage and cookies in your browser.

The reviewed account has Google Signals and user-provided data collection disabled, granular location and device collection enabled, and no linked Google Ads account. Optional account sharing for Google products and services, modeling contributions and business insights, and business recommendations is off; technical-support access is enabled. The owner has confirmed acceptance of Google’s data-processing terms. These settings do not activate advertising on this website. Any future advertising integration will have its own disclosures and applicable consent controls before it starts.

The event and user data retention periods are both fourteen months, with reset on new user activity off. The owner has confirmed that these settings were saved. These periods apply to the relevant user/event-level data and do not limit all standard aggregated reports. Analytics cookies have the separate, shorter lifetime described above. See Google’s retention documentation.

Processing may take place outside the EEA. Google’s data-processing terms describe the applicable international-transfer provisions and safeguards. See also the Google Analytics terms. Website analytics is optional and starts only after acceptance. You can withdraw your choice at any time using Analytics settings in the footer.

Your choices

  • You can avoid entering real player names.
  • You can delete saved matches or local app data. Manage system backups, migration copies and exported files separately.
  • You choose whether to export, save, print or share PDF reports and PNG images, and which destination receives them.
  • You can turn live-match notifications and Weekly article alerts on or off separately and use Android’s lock-screen privacy settings.
  • You can reject optional website analytics or withdraw through “Analytics settings”.
  • You can read Weekly without commenting or reacting and contact us about deletion of participation data.

Your privacy rights

Where applicable, you may request access to your personal data, correction or deletion of it, restriction of its processing, or object to its processing. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. For private-sector matters in Bavaria, the likely supervisory authority is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA).

No automated decision-making

We do not use automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you. Optional website analytics uses browser identifiers to understand website use; it does not make scoring decisions or provide the developer with native match history.

Children and younger users

CourtUmpire is intended for tennis players, coaches, and match organizers aged 13 and over. The app is not designed for children under 13.

Native scorekeeping requires no account and includes no advertising or analytics SDK. Explore loads the online services described above, including optional website analytics and Weekly participation. Younger users can reject analytics and read without participation; please do not submit unnecessary personal information about yourself or other players.

If the app is used by minors in training, club, school, or tournament contexts, users should avoid entering unnecessary personal information. Player names can be entered as first names, initials, nicknames, or temporary labels.

Scope of these choices

Native scoring, device backup, file sharing, Android notifications, website analytics and Weekly participation have different controls. Enabling one does not enable the others. If our processing changes, we will update this notice and request any additional consent required before optional tracking starts.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app or website changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.
